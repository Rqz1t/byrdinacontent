About me.

AnastasiiaWarsaw, Poland

Hi, I’m Anastasiia. I live in Warsaw and create photos and videos for beauty, fashion and lifestyle brands.

I love showing products in everyday moments: a bag on a city walk, skincare in a morning routine, a favourite accessory that brings an outfit together.

I work with Polish and international brands. From unboxing to showing a product in use, I choose a format that fits your idea and your brand’s style.

Natural light, thoughtful compositions and details worth a closer look — these are the things I love bringing to my work.
